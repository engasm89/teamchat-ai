# TeamChat AI: Technical Assessment

Start with `ASSESSMENT.md`. Read it end to end before you plan anything, and read
the data in `teamchat-ai-seed-data/` before you write your brief: a good deal of
what the product has to do is only visible in the files.

## What is here

- `ASSESSMENT.md` — the product story, the ground rules and how we evaluate
- `teamchat-ai-seed-data/` — the two chains' data, which the application loads at
  seed time and treats as the source of truth

## The shape of the week

This runs in two phases. First you distil the story into a technical brief and
send it to us. Then you build what the brief says you will build. The brief is
graded, and it is not a formality. `ASSESSMENT.md` sets out what it must contain.
